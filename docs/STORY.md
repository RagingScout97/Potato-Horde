# Potato Horde — Story

Short lore for menus, intro crawl, and chapter stubs. Gameplay stays move-only; the story is why you keep running.

## Logline

A crash-landed potato astronaut wakes up in a world that should not exist: endless potato-zombies, scrap-metal guns that fire themselves, and a flickering alliance of survivors who only survive by **getting stronger every minute**.

## Origin

You were cargo — a seed-bank pod headed for a quiet colony moon. Something tore the sky open. You hit dirt hard. When the hatch blew, the fields were already moving: soft yellow skins, empty eyes, teeth like broken fries.

They are not plants. They are **horde**.

## The apocalypse

Nobody agrees what started it. A bio-lab spill. A meme that became meat. A curse from an angry chef-god. What matters: the dead potatoes do not stop. Kill a hundred, a thousand more smell the starch. Night and day blur into one long **wave**.

## The alliance

Scattered green survivors (you), cyan allies who drop in by parachute, and whoever still holds a radio form a thin line. You do not conquer. You **buy time**. Banknotes, gear, and draft skills are the language of that alliance — grow ridiculous builds so the next runner lasts longer.

## Why endless

There is no final trench. Chapters push the front a few kilometers; Endless is the truth underneath: the horde scales forever, and so do you. The fight is addictive because every level-up is a tiny miracle — one more weapon, one more evolution, one more “just thirty seconds.” Clear fantasy, clear loop: **move, auto-fire, draft, survive**.

## Tone

Punchy, a little absurd, never cruel. Colored boxes are fine; the *feeling* is arcade survival with a potato heart.
